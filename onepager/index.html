What we do
Every AI agent today forgets. Membria is the layer that remembers why a decision was made — and what happened next.
One causal-memory core plugs into Claude Code, Codex and Cursor through MCP, and ships as vertical products for engineering (EPC), software teams and audit.
Problem
  • Agents are stateless — every session starts from zero, yesterday's reasoning is discarded.
  • In engineering, 28% of budget overruns are caused by rework; 30–50% of assumptions vanish at every handover.
  • A senior engineer leaves and fifteen years of judgment leaves with them.
Solution
  • Causal memory — Decision · Outcome · NegativeKnowledge · Skill in one graph.
  • Verification — five discipline critics check every P&ID against standards before sign-off.
  • One command to connect — npx @membria/cli init, no integration project.
