Large EPC companies run multi-billion dollar projects — and the knowledge needed to execute them is scattered across documents, disciplines and rotating teams.

An engineer re-solves a P&ID problem solved two projects ago. A compliance clause slips past a busy reviewer. A senior engineer leaves and takes fifteen years of judgment with them.

01
EPC is digitizing — but knowledge stays siloed
Complex EPC projects across oil & gas, petrochemical and industrial sectors each generate thousands of drawings, specifications and decisions that remain isolated per project.
The knowledge already exists — it just isn't connected
02
The missing layer is engineering intelligence
Each discipline has its tools. The opportunity is a governed layer that connects P&ID verification, standards compliance and cross-phase project knowledge — not just within a project, but across all of them.
Connected intelligence reduces rework and speeds delivery
03
The moment is now
EU AI Act and ISO 19650 revision create demand for traceable decisions and immutable audit trails in safety-critical infrastructure — exactly what Membria already delivers.
